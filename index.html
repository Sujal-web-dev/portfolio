The actual story

      
Hey! I'm Sujal Raj Gupta, a 2nd-year B.Tech student in Computer Science (Cyber Security) at Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology and Research (VFSTR), Vadlamudi, Guntur. I'm from Chas, Bokaro Steel City, Jharkhand, and I graduate in 2029.

      
I got into security because I wanted to understand how systems actually break. Every app and network we use runs on rules, and attackers look for the places where those rules fail. Learning to see those gaps, and then close them, felt like the most useful skill I could build.

      
I started with Linux, Python and C, then moved to tools like Nmap, Wireshark and Burp Suite on legal practice labs. I also try smaller things like steganography and password-auditing tools, because the best way I learn is by running something and seeing what happens.

      
I'm still early in this field, and I'd rather be honest about that than pretend otherwise. What I can promise is that I practise on TryHackMe, Hack The Box and LeetCode, I only test systems I have permission to test, and I keep learning.

Best platforms for a cyber security beginner

    
Places to learn, build a reputation and earn. Ordered by how well they fit a student starting out.

    

      
TryHackMe
Best start
Guided, browser-based rooms covering networking, Linux and web hacking.Finish a learning path and share your badge.

      
Hack The Box
Skill proof
Harder machines and challenges. A good rank impresses recruiters.Move here after the basics.

      
HackerOne
Bug bounty
Report vulnerabilities in programs that allow it and get paid for valid findings.Read disclosed reports first.

      
Bugcrowd
Bug bounty
Another large bug bounty platform, with beginner-friendly programs.Follow each program's scope strictly.

      
Upwork and Fiverr
Freelance
Sell small gigs such as Python security scripts, hardening guides and basic checks.Start with low prices to collect reviews.

      
LinkedIn
Networking
Post write-ups and projects weekly. Security teams and recruiters notice consistent learners.Turn on "Open to work".
